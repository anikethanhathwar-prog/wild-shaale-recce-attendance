Wild Shaale — School Recce

Offline-first school visit data collection

Open Setup while online to connect your destination Google Sheet.

Pre-Recce Setup

Enter the Apps Script Web App URL and the Google Sheet where recce records should be saved. Run setup before travelling.

Configure your form

After preparing the sheet, open it and add your location and question details to these tabs:

  • Location Master: Park Name, District Name, Taluka Name, Village Name
  • Form Configuration: Field ID, Field Label, Type, Required, Options, Section, Active
  • Educators: Educator Name, Active
  • Sync Log: Sync status records

After editing the configuration tabs, return here and select “Download / Refresh Configuration”. Do this while online.
